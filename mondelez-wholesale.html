Terms of Sale

Wholesale terms for Mondelez International corporate apparel programs.

1. Pricing & Currency

All quotes and invoices are denominated in United States Dollars (USD) unless otherwise agreed in writing. Published tier pricing applies at time of order confirmation. Volume breaks are calculated per SKU.

2. Minimum Orders

Standard minimum is 100 units per SKU. Outerwear minimum is 50 units per SKU. Mixed-size breakdowns must meet total SKU minimum.

3. Lead Times

Production lead times of 6 to 10 weeks begin upon final art approval and receipt of deposit (if applicable). Lead times are estimates and exclude transit to destination DC.

4. Art Approval

All decorated goods require approved virtual proofs before production. Client is responsible for reviewing proofs for accuracy. Approved proofs are binding for color, placement, and sizing.

5. Payment Terms

Approved accounts: Net 30 from invoice date. New accounts: 50% deposit at order, balance prior to shipment. Late payments subject to 1.5% monthly service charge.

6. Returns & Claims

Custom decorated apparel is non-returnable except for manufacturing defects. Claims must be submitted within 10 business days of receipt with photos and order reference.

Art Approval Process

Guidelines for submitting artwork and approving production proofs.

Step 1: Submit Artwork

Upload vector files (AI, EPS, or PDF) with outlined fonts. Include Pantone references for brand colors. Mondelez International logo usage must comply with corporate brand guidelines.

Step 2: Virtual Proof

Our art team produces a virtual proof within 3 business days. Proofs show logo placement, sizing, and color callouts on the actual garment template.

Step 3: Review & Approve

Designated approvers on your account review proofs in the Approvals section. Approve or request revisions with annotated feedback.

Step 4: Production

Once approved, the proof is locked and production begins. Any changes after approval may incur setup fees and delay lead time.

File Requirements

  • Vector format preferred (AI, EPS, PDF)
  • Minimum 300 DPI for raster elements
  • Pantone 268 C for brand purple
  • Maximum logo sizes per spec sheet on each product page
